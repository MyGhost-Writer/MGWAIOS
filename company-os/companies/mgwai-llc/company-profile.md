## Plain-English Description

MGWAI LLC builds practical AI-enabled software, automation systems, and company operating layers for businesses. The company combines software delivery, business process understanding, and agentic AI workflows to help owners turn scattered knowledge into useful systems.

## Current Business Avenues

- Custom software and ongoing software support retainers.
- AI business operating systems for companies.
- Travel business support through TNW Travel.
- Cruise Commander as a potential travel app and agent interface.
- Ghostwriter as an AI book-writing application.
- Internal training applications.
- Data pipeline and system-to-system integration tools.

## Positioning

MGWAI should be known for building useful, owner-aware AI systems that actually fit how a business works.

The company is not just selling generic chatbots. It is building company-aware workflows, software, and automation backed by clear operating structure.

## Customers

Primary customer types:

- Small business owners
- Service companies
- Travel customers and travel operators
- Teams with manual data movement or training needs
- Organizations that need custom internal tools

Secondary customer types:

- Authors and creators
- Agencies
- Companies with messy knowledge systems
- Businesses looking for AI adoption without hiring a full internal team

Best-fit customer:

A business owner who has real operational knowledge, repeatable work, and enough pain from manual processes to value a tailored AI/software system.

Poor-fit customer:

A customer who wants vague AI magic without providing context, access, review, or a clear business outcome.

## Operating Model

MGWAI should operate as a software delivery and AI operating system company.

The reusable MGWAIOS core should be separated from MGWAI LLC's own company instance so the same structure can be installed for clients.

## Approval Rules

AI may:

- Draft proposals, workflows, outlines, internal docs, and software plans.
- Analyze company context and recommend structure.
- Create task packets and agent recipes.
- Build code when explicitly asked.

AI must ask before:

- Sending anything to a client.
- Making pricing commitments.
- Booking travel.
- Publishing public content.
- Connecting to sensitive systems.
- Making legal, compliance, HR, or financial determinations.

AI must never:

- Pretend unverified facts are confirmed.
- Delete or overwrite client data without explicit authorization.
- Use client data in another company's instance.
- Treat temporary worker context as durable memory unless it is written back through approved memory rules.
