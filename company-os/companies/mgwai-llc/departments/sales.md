# Sales Department

## Purpose

Turn business opportunities into clear, scoped, paid work.

## Responsibilities

- Qualify leads.
- Draft proposals.
- Define activation fees and recurring retainers.
- Maintain follow-up workflows.
- Translate client pain into software or automation offers.

## Inputs

- Client conversations
- Intake notes
- Existing service offers
- Pricing rules
- Case studies

## Outputs

- Proposals
- Scopes of work
- Follow-up messages
- Pricing recommendations
- Sales pipeline notes

## Agent Opportunities

- Lead qualifier
- Proposal writer
- Follow-up writer
- Scope builder

## Approval Rules

AI can draft sales materials.

Owner approval is required before sending proposals, making price commitments, or promising delivery timelines.
