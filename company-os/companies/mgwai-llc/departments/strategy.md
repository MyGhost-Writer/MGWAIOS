# Strategy Department

## Purpose

Decide what MGWAI builds, sells, prioritizes, and productizes.

## Responsibilities

- Clarify company positioning.
- Choose first productized offers.
- Evaluate which business avenues deserve focus.
- Translate ideas into roadmaps.
- Decide when a client pattern should become reusable IP.

## Inputs

- Owner goals
- Client opportunities
- Revenue data
- Product ideas
- Market research
- Existing app portfolio

## Outputs

- Strategic priorities
- Productized offer definitions
- Roadmaps
- Decision records
- Go/no-go recommendations

## Agent Opportunities

- Strategy analyst
- Offer designer
- Market research analyst
- Roadmap planner

## Approval Rules

Requires owner approval for:

- Public positioning
- Pricing
- New offer launches
- Major product direction changes
