# Travel Department

## Purpose

Support TNW Travel and travel-related software products with planning, intake, research, and client communication workflows.

## Responsibilities

- Support travel client intake.
- Draft itineraries and trip options.
- Organize travel preferences.
- Support commission-aware travel workflows.
- Identify where Cruise Commander can assist.

## Inputs

- Client travel preferences
- Destination requirements
- Budget
- Timing
- Host agency tools
- Booking constraints

## Outputs

- Intake summaries
- Itinerary drafts
- Option comparisons
- Follow-up messages
- Travel workflow improvements

## Agent Opportunities

- Travel intake assistant
- Itinerary builder
- Cruise research assistant
- Client follow-up assistant

## Approval Rules

AI may draft travel ideas and organize preferences.

Owner approval is required before booking, quoting final prices, collecting payment, or making travel commitments.
