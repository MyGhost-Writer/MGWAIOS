# Lead To Proposal Workflow

## Purpose

Turn a potential client conversation into a clear proposal with scope, pricing, and next steps.

## Trigger

A new client expresses interest in custom software, automation, MGWAIOS, travel support, or another MGWAI offer.

## End State

A reviewed proposal is ready for the owner to send.

## Steps

1. Capture intake notes.
2. Identify the relevant offer.
3. Clarify the desired business outcome.
4. Identify constraints, timeline, systems, and budget.
5. Select a proposal writer agent recipe.
6. Create a task packet.
7. Draft proposal.
8. Review scope, pricing, risks, and assumptions.
9. Owner approves or revises.
10. Save final proposal and relevant lessons to memory.

## Inputs

- Client name
- Business description
- Problem statement
- Desired outcome
- Budget or pricing model
- Timeline
- Systems involved

## Outputs

- Proposal draft
- Scope assumptions
- Pricing notes
- Follow-up message

## Approval Points

- Pricing
- Delivery timeline
- Client-facing proposal
- Contract language

## Agent Roles

- Lead qualifier
- Proposal writer
- Scope reviewer

## Risks

- Overscoping early work.
- Making commitments before system access is understood.
- Underpricing ongoing support.
